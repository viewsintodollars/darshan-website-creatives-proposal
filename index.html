Website Creatives · Scope Boundaries & Terms

What the graphics cover, what sits outside them, and the terms.

In scope

  • Style frame and a 3 SKU pilot
  • 5 images per SKU for 146 SKUs
  • Mobile crops of every image
  • Batch delivery in step with SKU onboarding
  • Two revision rounds per batch
  • Source and web ready files, named by SKU

Out of scope

  • Photography, studio and location shoots
  • Packaging or label redesign
  • Video and animation
  • Marketplace listing images: Listings proposal
  • Ad creatives: Performance Ads proposal
  • Banners, unless ordered per piece

Commercial terms

  • Priced per SKU; more SKUs at the same rate
  • Client supplies pack photos or artwork files
  • Product facts on images come from Client approved data
  • Changes after batch sign off charged per image
  • 50% at kick off, balance per delivered batch
  • Fees exclude GST
